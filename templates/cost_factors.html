How It Works

The AI optimization uses these factors to calculate:

  • Fuel Cost: Distance x 2 (round trip) / MPG x Price
  • Time Cost: Drive time + load/unload time
  • Labor Cost: Total hours x Driver rate
  • Equipment Cost: Total hours x Truck rate

Total Cost = Fuel + Labor + Equipment

Example Calculation

25 mile delivery:

  • Fuel: 50mi / 6mpg x $3.50 = $29.17
  • Time: 1.4h drive + 0.6h load = 2h
  • Labor: 2h x $25 = $50.00
  • Truck: 2h x $15 = $30.00

Total: $109.17
